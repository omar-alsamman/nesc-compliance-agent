# NESC Reference Excerpts (condensed)

Working excerpt used by the rule engine and, eventually, the retrieval
layer. Not a substitute for the full NESC -- cite the actual code section
for anything going into a real engineering deliverable.

- Rule 092 (Grounding): Grounding system resistance shall not exceed
-   25 ohm, measured per the applicable test method.
-   - Rule 096C (Neutral grounding): Multi-grounded neutral systems require
    -   grounding at a minimum frequency of 4 times per mile.
    -   - Rule 232, Table 232-1 (Clearances): Minimum vertical clearance
        -   requirements for conductors over ground, roads, and other features --
        -     varies by voltage class and crossing type.
        - - Rule 250B (Loading districts): Structures shall be designed for the
          -   governing NESC loading district (light/medium/heavy) plus any
          -     project-specific extreme wind condition.
          - - Rule 261/263 (Grade of construction): Grade C construction applies to
            -   most residential overhead distribution; governs required strength
            -     factors for poles, conductors, and hardware.
            - - Rule 261H (Guys and anchors): Guy and anchor assemblies shall be
              -   installed per manufacturer requirements and provide adequate capacity
              -     for the calculated guy tension with an appropriate safety factor.
